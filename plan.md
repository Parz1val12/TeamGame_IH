**Game name:**

    Yeti Climber

**Platformer or RPG?**

    Platformer

**What is your game about? Who is the player, and where are they?**

    Climbing a snowy Mt. | Yeti | On a snowy Mt.

**What does the player do? (Run and jump? Explore? Collect things? Avoid things?)**

    Go higher and avoid danger like enemies or spikes

**What are the controls?**

    Jump: space, w, up arrow
    Left: a, left arrow
    Right: d, right arrow

**How do you win?**

    Reach the top

**How do you lose?**

    Falling and other dangers along the way.
